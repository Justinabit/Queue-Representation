OperationTimeWhat it does
enqueue(x)O(1)Places x at index rear+1, then moves rear forward.
dequeue()O(1)Reads the value at front, then moves front forward.
peek()O(1)Reads the value at front without moving anything.
traversalO(n)Visits every slot from front to rear, in order.
isEmpty()O(1)True when front > rear (no active slots).
